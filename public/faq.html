Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about using GrowWith Billing.

What is GrowWith Billing?

GrowWith Billing is a billing and product-management app for small businesses. Use it to organise products, create bills, scan supported product codes, and review sales information.

Can I add and manage my own products?

Yes. Add product details such as name, SKU, size, colour, price, and available stock. You can search your product list by name or SKU.

How do I create a bill?

Open Billing, find a product by name or scan its supported code, and add it to the bill. Customer name and phone number are optional.

Do I need a separate barcode scanner?

No separate scanner is required for the in-app scan feature. You can also search for products by name or SKU instead.

Is GrowWith Billing free?

Check the app or contact us for current plan, pricing, and availability details. Any applicable charges will be shown before you purchase a paid service.

Is my business data backed up automatically?

Data storage and synchronization depend on the service configuration and your account. Keep your own copies of important business records. See our Privacy Policy for more information about storage and retention.

How can I delete my account?

For a signed-in account, use the account deletion option in the app and confirm the request with your password. When the request succeeds, the app requests deletion of your account and associated records from the active database. Guest data stored locally on your device and copies you exported or saved elsewhere are separate and are not deleted by this action. You can also follow the instructions on our account deletion page. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account or cloud data.
