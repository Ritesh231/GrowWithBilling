GrowWith Billing

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This policy explains how GrowWith Billing handles information when you use our billing and product-management app. It applies to the app and related support. The app is intended for business use; you are responsible for providing appropriate notices to your customers before entering their information.

Information the app handles

Depending on the features you use and the information you enter, GrowWith Billing may handle:

  • Account information: information used to create and identify your account, such as your name, email address, and sign-in or account identifiers.
  • Customer information: customer names, phone numbers, and addresses that you choose to add to your business records.
  • Billing and transaction records: invoices or bills, line items, quantities, prices, totals, dates, and related sales or business records.
  • Product and inventory information: product names, SKUs or codes, descriptions, prices, sizes, colours, and stock counts.
  • Technical and support information: information you send when contacting support and basic technical information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the app.

How information is used

We use this information to create and manage your account, store and synchronize your business records, prepare bills, update product and inventory information, show sales summaries, provide support, maintain the service, and protect it from misuse. We do not use customer or business records for unrelated advertising.

Storage and synchronization

App information may be stored locally on your device and synchronized with our cloud service. Our backend is hosted by Hostinger, and the cloud database is hosted by MongoDB Atlas. Hostinger processes information sent to the backend to operate the service; MongoDB Atlas stores cloud-synchronized app records. Synchronization and the information available in the cloud depend on your use of the app and its configuration. Protect your device and account credentials, and keep separate copies of records that your business must retain.

Camera and barcode scanning

The app requests camera access when you choose to use the barcode scan feature. Camera images and barcode scan results are processed on your device only; they are not uploaded to our servers or retained by GrowWith Billing. You can search for products by name or SKU instead of using the camera.

When information is shared

We do not sell your personal or business information. Information may be disclosed only as needed to:

  • Hostinger, to host and operate the app backend;
  • MongoDB Atlas, as the cloud database hosting provider, to store and synchronize app data;
  • respond to your support requests using the communication service involved in your message;
  • comply with applicable law, a valid legal request, or protect users, the service, and our rights; or
  • support a business transfer, if one occurs, subject to appropriate protections and applicable law.

These providers process information to provide their services and may have their own privacy terms. No analytics or diagnostic SDKs are currently active in the app.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. No app, device, or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Use a strong sign-in method, keep your device secure, and avoid entering payment-card details or passwords in customer or invoice fields.

Retention and deletion

You can delete a signed-in account in the app by submitting an account deletion request and confirming it with your password. You can also request deletion using the account deletion page. We may ask you to verify account ownership before acting on an email request.

When an in-app deletion request succeeds, the app requests deletion of the account and its associated records from the active database. This describes the app and backend deletion behavior; the live result depends on the deployed backend processing the request successfully. We aim to process verified email deletion requests within 1–2 days.

Guest data stored locally on your device is separate and is not deleted when you delete a signed-in account. Manual exports and other copies saved elsewhere are also not deleted by the app. Uninstalling the app does not by itself delete an account or cloud-synchronized information.

Children

GrowWith Billing is a business tool and is not intended for children. Do not create an account for a child or enter children’s personal information unless legally authorized to do so.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at ritesh.tamboli40@gmail.com or visit our Contact Us page. To raise a complaint, include “Privacy complaint” in the subject and describe the issue without including passwords or sign-in codes.
