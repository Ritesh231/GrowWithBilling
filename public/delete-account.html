Request account deletion

You can request deletion of your GrowWith Billing account and associated personal and business data from this page. This request is for the account owner.

How to submit a request

  1. Send an email from the email address associated with your GrowWith Billing account.
  2. Include “Account deletion request” in the subject and provide the account email address. Do not send your password or payment credentials.
  3. We may contact you to verify account ownership before processing the request.

Email a deletion request

For a signed-in account, you can also use the account deletion option in the app and confirm the request with your password. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete your account or cloud data.

We aim to process a verified email request within 1–2 days. If we need more information to confirm account ownership, we will contact you; the processing timeframe begins once verification is complete.

What is deleted

When an in-app deletion request succeeds, the app requests deletion of the signed-in account and its associated records from the active database. This describes the app and backend deletion behavior; the live result depends on the deployed backend processing the request successfully.

Guest data stored locally on your device is separate and is not deleted by deleting a signed-in account. The app also does not delete manual exports or other copies you saved elsewhere. Keep any records you need before deleting your account.
