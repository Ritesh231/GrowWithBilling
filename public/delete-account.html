Request account deletion

You can request deletion of your GrowWith Billing account and associated personal and business data from this page. This request is for the account owner.

How to submit a request

  1. Send an email from the email address associated with your GrowWith Billing account.
  2. Include “Account deletion request” in the subject and provide the account email address. Do not send your password or payment credentials.
  3. We may contact you to verify account ownership before processing the request.

Email a deletion request

If your version of the app has an account deletion option, you may also submit a request there. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete your account or cloud data.

We aim to process a verified request within 1–2 days. If we need more information to confirm account ownership, we will contact you; the processing timeframe begins once verification is complete.

What is deleted

After we verify and process your request, we delete the account and its associated customer, product, inventory, billing, invoice, and transaction information from active systems, except where particular information must be retained for a legal, dispute-resolution, or security purpose. Where data is retained for such a reason, access is restricted and it is kept only for as long as necessary.

Copies held in backups may remain for up to 15 days after deletion from active systems. Information stored only on your device may need to be removed separately from the device after account deletion.
