Request account deletion

You can request deletion of your GrowWith Billing account and associated personal and business data from this page. This request is for the account owner.

How to submit a request

  1. Send an email from the email address associated with your GrowWith Billing account.
  2. Include “Account deletion request” in the subject and provide the account email address. Do not send your password or payment credentials.
  3. We may contact you to verify account ownership before processing the request.

Email a deletion request

If your device has an account deletion option, you may also submit a request there. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete your account or cloud data.

What is deleted

After we verify and complete your request, we delete or de-identify the account information and associated customer, product, inventory, billing, invoice, and transaction information linked to the account from active systems, except where particular information must be retained for a legal, dispute-resolution, or security purpose. Where data is retained for such a reason, access is restricted and it is kept only for as long as necessary.

Copies held in backups may remain until those backups are overwritten or expire. A fixed backup-retention period is not currently stated. Information stored only on your device may need to be removed separately from the device after account deletion.
