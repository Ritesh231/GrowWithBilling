How to get useful support

To help us understand your question, include the relevant details in your message:

  • A short description of what you were trying to do and what happened.
  • The app version and your device type, if your question is about a technical issue.
  • Any steps that help us reproduce the issue. You may attach a screenshot, but remove personal or business information that is not needed.

For your security, do not email your password, sign-in codes, payment-card details, or other sensitive credentials.
